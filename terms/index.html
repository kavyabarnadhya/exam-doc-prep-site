1. What the app is

A tool that resizes and compresses photos and signatures to file-size and pixel limits, and reports what it measured in the file it saved.

2. Independent app

The app is independent. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, sponsored by or run by any exam body, including UPSC, SSC, NTA and IBPS. Exam names are used only to describe which published rules a setting is based on.

3. Exam rules can be wrong or out of date

  • Rules shown in the app come from published exam notices and, where marked, from education websites. Each rule shows its source and date.
  • Some rules are marked as examples from a past cycle and are not current.
  • Exam bodies can change their rules at any time, and the app may not have the change.
  • Always check the current notice from the exam body before you upload.

4. No promise of acceptance

  • A "Fits" result means the saved file met the limits shown in the app when it was measured.
  • It does not mean an exam portal will accept the file. Only the exam body decides that.
  • The app checks file size, format and pixel dimensions. It does not decide whether your photo meets other requirements such as background, face size, clothing, date or name on the photo.

5. Your responsibilities

  • Use only images you have the right to use.
  • Look at the result before you upload it, and make sure it is clear and meets all the requirements of your exam.
  • Keep your original image. The app does not change it, but you are responsible for your own copies.
