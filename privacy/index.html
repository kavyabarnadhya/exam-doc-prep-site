Exam Doc Prep — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026

Summary

Exam Doc Prep resizes and compresses photos and signatures on your phone. It does not upload your images, and it has no accounts and no analytics. It uses the internet for two things only: to download a file of exam photo rules, and to show one advertisement on its Home screen, supplied by Google AdMob. Ads are never shown while you work on a document, and every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment, so ads are not personalised and the advertising ID is not sent.

1. Your images

  • You choose an image from your phone. The app reads it and makes a new, smaller file. This work is done on your phone and does not need an internet connection.
  • The app does not change or delete the image you chose.
  • The app does not upload or send your images anywhere on its own.
  • An image leaves the app only when you tap Save to Gallery, Save as file or Share:
    • Save to Gallery (Android 10 and later) puts a copy in the Pictures/ExamDocPrep folder on your phone. Like other pictures there, it can be seen by apps that you have given access to your photos, and it may be included in any photo backup you have turned on in another app.
    • Save as file puts a copy in the place you choose.
    • Share passes a copy to the app you choose. What that app does with it is covered by that app's own privacy policy.

2. What the app keeps on your phone

  • The most recent file the app made. It is kept in the app's private storage so you can save or share it. It is replaced when you make a new file. Saving or sharing does not remove it.
  • The exam rules file, its digital signature, and a small record of when the app last checked for new rules (the times of the last check and the last successful check, the number of failed checks in a row, and a version tag for the rules file).
  • While you work on an image, the app and Android may hold temporary copies in the app's private storage or memory.
  • All of the above is removed when you clear the app's data or uninstall the app. Copies you saved to Gallery, saved as a file or shared are not removed; you manage those yourself.

3. Information the app does not ask for or collect

  • No name, email address, phone number, age or login.
  • No location or contacts.
  • The app contains no analytics or crash-reporting software. The only third-party software that sends data is the advertising software described in section 5.

4. Internet use

  • The app downloads two small files from its rules website, https://kavyabarnadhya.github.io/exam-doc-prep-site/ : the exam rules and a signature used to check that the rules file has not been altered.
  • When this happens: the app checks when you open it, normally at most once every 24 hours. If a check fails, it tries again later (after 1 hour, then 6 hours, then 24 hours). The "Check for updates" button works at most once every 5 minutes.
  • What the request contains: no images, no account, no cookies and no device identifier set by the app. Like any web request, it shows the website's host your IP address and the time of the request. It also carries the app's name ("ExamDocPrep-rules") and, once the rules have been downloaded before, a version tag for the rules file so that an unchanged file is not downloaded again.
  • The website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc. GitHub may log these request details; its handling of them is described in GitHub's own privacy statement.
  • The only other internet connections are those made by the advertising software described in section 5.

5. Advertising

  • The app shows one banner advertisement at the bottom of its Home screen. It shows no ads on any other screen, and no full-screen ads.
  • Ads are provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC, through the Google Mobile Ads software in the app.
  • Every ad request is marked for child-directed treatment and limited to ads rated suitable for general audiences. Under this setting, Google states that personalised ads and remarketing are turned off, requests to third-party ad vendors are turned off, and the Android advertising ID is not sent. The app also removes the advertising ID permissions from Android.
  • To show and protect ads, Google's software may collect and share with Google: your device's IP address (which may be used to estimate its general location), interactions with the ad (such as when the ad is shown or tapped), information about how the app and the ad software perform, and an app set ID. Google describes this in its data disclosure for the Google Mobile Ads software.
  • Your images and the files the app makes are never given to the advertising software.
  • Where the law requires it (for example in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before any ad is requested. If consent is not given, or the app cannot check, no ad is shown. You can change your choice at any time under "Ad privacy choices" in the app's Help screen, when that option applies where you are.
  • If the app is offline, or the ad cannot load, no ad is shown and the app works as normal.
  • Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

6. Backups

  • The app's private storage is excluded from Android cloud backup and from device-to-device transfer.
  • This does not apply to copies you save to Gallery or as a file; those follow your phone's own backup settings.

7. Permissions

  • The app asks for internet access, for the rules download and the advertisement described above. The advertising software also adds permissions to check whether the phone is online and to finish its background work; these do not give access to your images or personal information.
  • The app removes the advertising ID permissions that the advertising software would otherwise add.
  • It does not ask for camera, location, contacts or storage permission. You choose images through Android's file picker, which gives the app access only to the image you pick.

8. Children

  • The app can be used by people of any age, including children, for example a student preparing documents for a school entrance form with a parent's help.
  • The app does not ask anyone's age and does not ask for personal information from any user.
  • Because the app does not know who is using it, every ad request is treated as coming from a child, as described in section 5.
  • A parent or guardian with questions can contact us at the address below.
